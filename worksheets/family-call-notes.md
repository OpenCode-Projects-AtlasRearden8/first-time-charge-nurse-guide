# Family-Call Notes Sheet

**Education only — this field manual is never medical advice.** A planning sheet for the non-clinical half of family and caller communication (chapter 5). Blank shell by design: no caller names, no room numbers tied to people, no clinical content ever. Record *requests and promises only*, exactly as you would on the shared desk pad.

## The 2-sentence intake (every incoming call)

```text
"Charge desk — can I get a number where I'll call you back
 if we get disconnected?"
"Give me the first-name-only and which room you're asking about,
 and I'll see what I can tell you."
```

## Which calls are mine vs. the room's

| Mine at the desk — logistics | The room nurse's or provider's — clinical |
|---|---|
| Schedule-plan status ("no change to report") | Status, plan, results — anything about care |
| Timing, visitors-in-general, parking/facilities | Symptoms and what they mean |
| Who is coming (the shift's staffing shape) | Every "what does this mean" sentence |

**The hand-off script, always offered, never forced:** *"Anything about their medical care, I'd have to connect the nurse — want me to do that?"*

**Promise kept late beats promise never — and a promise made twice is a system problem: write it once, keep it once.**

## Situations and their script shapes

| Situation | First sentence shape | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| Routine update request | "I can tell you where things stand schedule-wise…" | Anything clinical "just this once" |
| Why hasn't anyone called? | "I'm sorry — I'll find out and have someone call you back." | defending · scolding the colleague aloud |
| The angry caller | "I hear you — here's what I can do from the desk:" | matching volume · editorializing about colleagues · promising outcomes |
| "Whoever is in charge" | "That's me right now — what can I help with?" | the defensive laugh |
| The vigil call (slow voice, logistics) | "I'm here — let me take this one step at a time." | rushing · any clinical timeline I don't have |
| Second promise collides with first | "Two things promised from this desk — you get the first callback." | pretending one didn't happen |

## The 20-minute callback promise

> When I can't resolve it now: "Let me check where things stand and call you back in twenty minutes, with your number." — then *fire the callback even if the update is partial.*
