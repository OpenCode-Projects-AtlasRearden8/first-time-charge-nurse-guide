# End-of-Shift Debrief Sheet

**Education only — this field manual is never medical advice.** Chapter 8's last-forty-minutes routine on one sheet: handoff shape, thank-down, and the five-minute personal debrief. Blank shell by design — write about shapes, not about people; anything that would identify a patient or a coworker stays in your facility's systems.

## 1 · Close the loose ends (−40 to −25 min)

- ☐ Board state checked and written down
- ☐ Callback pad: every promise cleared or handed off by name
- ☐ Audit numbers ready for handoff (waiters / open-clean / openings)
- ☐ Escalations I made, written as one line each

## 2 · Charge-to-charge handoff (state → pressure → people)

```text
1 "Board state: ______, as of ______."
2 "Pressure: ______."
3 "Staffing: ______."
4 "Assignments to watch out for (workload shape, never opinions): ______."
5 "Open loops I'm handing you, by name and time: ______."
6 "The one thing to know before you sit down: ______."
```

## 3 · Thank-down (specific beats generic)

- One specific observable thing to the whole group: ______
- Two people by name on the way out, two seconds each: ______ ☐ ☐
- Coaching parked (not at the door): "grab a coffee this week — trade notes" ☐

## 4 · The five-minute personal debrief (one change, no more)

| Question | My answer (shapes and count, never names) |
|---|---|
| **State:** worst board moment — did my audit numbers predict it? | |
| **Ask:** one thing I escalated earlier than comfortable: | |
| **Ask:** one thing I held too long: | |
| **Fair:** one grid trade I would redo, and the reason I'd say out loud: | |
| **Next:** my ONE change for the next charge shift: | |

## 5 · If the shift held a hard moment

- ☐ Opened the door to my facility's formal debriefing process (they lead it — not a charger's script)
- ☐ One small sentence at handoff so the next charge walks in kindly
- ☐ Personal debrief, only the questions that felt possible
- ☐ Reached for my people later, off the floor

**One change per shift. That is the whole system. A debrief with five changes is five things to abandon by week three.**
