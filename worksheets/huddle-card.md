# Huddle Pocket Card

**Education only — this field manual is never medical advice.** The five-minute huddle from chapter 3, plus the 60-second micro-huddle, on one printable card. Fill-in slots use placeholders only — nothing here belongs to any person; names and details live on your facility's board.

## The 5-minute huddle (read it, don't recite it)

```text
01 "Huddle — five minutes, everyone. Gather up."
02 "Staff: ______ and ______. Floats: ______. Watches: ______."
03 "Board: ___ openings before ___:00, ___ waiters in house,
     likely ___ admits. I'll keep the desk updated as it changes."
04 "Watch-fors: ______  ______  ______"
05 "Breaks: your break is promised ______. Cover per the grid —
     the desk covers gaps that become unsafe."
06 "Questions? Grab me at the desk. Break."
```

**Watch-for menu (sentence 04):** planned room movement ☐ · supply/timing gaps ☐ · equipment issues ☐ · call-light-pattern shape ☐ · one human kindness ☐

## The 60-second micro-huddle (anytime the board lurches)

```text
"Not the day we planned. The board now: ______.
 What changes for the next two hours: ______.
 I need one thing from everyone: ______. Go."
```

## The 2-sentence thank-down (end of shift)

```text
"Thank you for today — specifically ______.
 Hand off per usual and get out of here safe."
```

## Huddle failure fixes

| Shape | Fix |
|---|---|
| Monologue (9 things, 0 questions) | Back to 6 sentences; questions to the desk |
| Vent circle about anyone | "Park it — see me directly." Move on |
| Empty room | Huddle the next bodies in; never skip fully |
| Repeat announcements | Whiteboard the stale ones; huddle only fresh facts |
